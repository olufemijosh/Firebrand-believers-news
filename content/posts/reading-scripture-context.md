When reading a Bible passage, begin by asking who is speaking, to whom, and in what situation. Notice the verses before and after the section you are studying. Consider the type of writing—poetry, history, parable, letter, or prophecy—and compare interpretations carefully rather than relying on a sentence taken out of context.

Prayer and study belong together. Consult reliable study resources, listen to thoughtful perspectives, and be willing to revisit assumptions. The goal is not simply to find a phrase that supports what we already believe, but to grow in understanding and apply Scripture with humility.
