God Cannot Be Mocked 🙏✨

For decades, the world marveled at the brilliant mind of Stephen Hawking. He unlocked secrets of the universe, peered into black holes, and spoke with an authority that captivated millions. Yet, in his immense intellect, he came to a definitive conclusion: “There is no God.” To him, the universe was a clockwork mechanism that required no Creator.

But the laws of the universe are bound by a timeline that none of us can escape.

In 2018, the great physicist’s time on this earth came to an end. The mind that denied the Creator stood before the ultimate reality. While man can spend a lifetime declaring that God does not exist, a single breath separates our earthly opinions from eternal truth.

The Bible reminds us in Galatians 6:7, "Do not be deceived: God cannot be mocked. A man reaps what he sows."

Science can explain the how of creation, but it can never replace the Who. Let us admire the brilliance of human discovery, but never lose our reverence for the One who gave us the minds to discover it in the first place.
