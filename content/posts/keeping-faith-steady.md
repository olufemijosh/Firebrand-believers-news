Uncertainty can test the habits that keep faith rooted. In difficult seasons, make room for prayer, return to Scripture thoughtfully, and stay connected to people who offer wise and compassionate encouragement. Faith does not require pretending that every question has an easy answer. It invites us to bring our questions honestly before God while continuing to act with love, courage, and integrity.

Choose one steady practice for today: read a short passage slowly, check on someone who may be struggling, or write down three things for which you are grateful. Small acts of faithfulness can help us face a complicated world without surrendering hope.
