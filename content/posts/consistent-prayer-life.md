A consistent prayer life does not have to begin with a complicated routine. Choose a realistic time, remove a distraction, and speak honestly about gratitude, fear, need, and hope. A short daily practice that you can sustain may be more helpful than an ambitious plan that quickly becomes a burden.

You might use a simple rhythm: be still, give thanks, name your concerns, pray for others, and reflect on a passage of Scripture. There will be days when prayer feels easy and days when words are hard to find. Keep returning with honesty rather than measuring your faith by a single moment.
