Christian living is not limited to Sunday services or public declarations. It is also reflected in honesty, patience, humility, forgiveness, and the way we treat people who cannot repay us. Everyday faith asks us to listen before reacting, tell the truth even when it costs us, and make room for mercy without abandoning wisdom.

Start with the relationships closest to you. Offer a sincere apology where needed. Keep a promise. Give someone your full attention. These ordinary decisions can make the values we profess more visible in the world.
